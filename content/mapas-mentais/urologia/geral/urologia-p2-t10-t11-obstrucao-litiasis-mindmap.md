## Estrutura do Mapa

**Urological Obstruction and Lithiasis**
  - ### Urinary Obstruction
    - Pathophysiology
      - Increased hydrostatic pressure
      - Hydronephrosis
      - Renal atrophy
    - Classification by Origin
      - Congenital (VUP, UPJ stenosis)
      - Adquired (Calculi, BPH, Tumors)
      - Functional (Neurogenic bladder)
    - Clinical Levels
      - High (Kidney/Ureter)
        - Flank pain
        - Renal colic
      - Low (Bladder/Urethra)
        - Suprapubic pain
        - Hesitancy
        - Urinary retention
    - Posterior Urethral Valve (VUP)
      - Congenital cause
      - Male newborns
      - Endoscopic fulguration
  - ### Urinary Lithiasis
    - Types of Calculi
      - Calcium (80%)
        - Oxalate or Phosphate
        - Radiopaque
      - Struvite (15%)
        - Staghorn/Coraliform shape
        - Urease-positive UTI (Proteus)
      - Uric Acid (5-8%)
        - Radiolucent (Invisible on X-ray)
        - Associated with Gout
      - Cistine (1-2%)
        - Hereditary (COLA)
        - Hexagonal crystals
    - Diagnosis
      - Non-contrast CT (Gold Standard)
      - Ultrasound (1st line for kids/pregnant)
      - KUB X-ray
    - Renal Colic Symptoms
      - Sudden intense pain
      - Patient agitation
      - Hematuria
      - Tachycardia
  - ### Treatment & Management
    - Conservative
      - NSAIDs (Diclofenac)
      - Tamsulosin (Expulsive therapy)
      - Hydration
    - Interventional
      - Emergency drainage (Nefrostomy)
      - ESWL (LEOC)
      - Ureteroscopy
      - PCNL (Nefrolitotomy)
    - Prophylaxis
      - High fluid intake
      - Salt restriction
      - Potassium citrate
