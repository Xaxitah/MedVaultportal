## Estrutura do Mapa

**Renal Tumors**
  - ### Classification
    - Cystic
      - Simple Renal Cyst
      - Polycystic Kidney Disease (PKD)
    - Solid
      - Benign (Adenoma, Oncocitoma, AML)
      - Malignant (CCR, Wilms)
  - ### Benign Tumors
    - Simple Renal Cyst
      - Most frequent benign tumor
      - Bosniak Classification (I-IV)
    - Angiomiolipoma (AML)
      - Fat on CT (-10 UH)
      - Tuberous Sclerosis association
      - Bleeding risk if > 4cm
    - Oncocitoma
      - Central scar (Bicycle wheel sign)
      - Differentiate from CCR
    - Renal Adenoma
      - Most common solid benign
      - Indistinguishable from CCR on imaging
  - ### Renal Cell Carcinoma (CCR)
    - Epidemiology
      - Adults 50-80 years
      - Smoking risk factor
    - Histological Subtypes
      - Clear Cell (70-80%, VHL mutation)
      - Papillary
      - Chromophobe
      - Bellini Ducts (Aggressive)
    - Clinical Presentation
      - Classic Triad: Hematuria, Pain, Mass
      - Paraneoplastic Syndromes
    - Diagnosis & Staging
      - Gold standard: CT with contrast
      - Fuhrman/ISUP Grade
      - TNM Staging
    - Treatment
      - Partial Nephrectomy (T1)
      - Radical Nephrectomy (T2-T3)
      - Immunotherapy/TKI (Metastatic)
  - ### Pediatric: Tumor de Wilms
    - Most common in children < 5y
    - Painless abdominal mass
    - No biopsy before surgery (seeding risk)
    - Genetic syndromes: WAGR, Beckwith-Wiedemann
  - ### Clinical Rules
    - Solid mass = Malignant until proven otherwise
    - Hematuria > 50y = Cancer until proven otherwise
