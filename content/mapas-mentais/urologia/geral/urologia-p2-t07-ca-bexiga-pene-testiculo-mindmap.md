## Estrutura do Mapa

**Urological Tumors (Bladder, Penis, and Testicle)**
  - ### Bladder Cancer
    - Epidemiology
      - Most common urinary tract tumor
      - Risk Factor: Smoking (50% of cases)
      - 94% Transitional Cell Carcinoma (UCT)
    - Diagnosis
      - Symptom: Painless hematuria
      - Gold Standard: Cystoscopy + Biopsy
      - Urinary Cytology
    - Classification & Treatment
      - NIUB (Non-Muscle Invasive)
        - Stages: Ta, Tis, T1
        - Treatment: RTU-V + BCG Intravesical
      - MIB (Muscle Invasive)
        - Stages: T2, T3, T4
        - Treatment: Radical Cystectomy + Neoadjuvant QMT
        - Urinary Diversion: Bricker or Studer
    - Other Histologies
      - Squamous Cell (Schistosoma haematobium)
      - Adenocarcinoma (Urachal)
  - ### Testicular Tumors
    - Epidemiology
      - Age: 15-35 years old
      - Risk: Cryptorchidism
    - Clinical Profile
      - Hard, painless mass
      - Markers: AFP, B-HCG, LDH
      - Diagnosis: Testicular Ultrasound
    - Management
      - Orquiectomia Inguinal Radical (Standard)
      - NEVER perform transcrotal biopsy
      - Seminoma: Radiosensitive
      - Non-Seminoma: Chemotherapy (BEP)
  - ### Penile Cancer
    - Etiology
      - 95% Squamous Cell Carcinoma
      - Risk: Phimosis and poor hygiene
      - Virus: HPV 16 and 18
    - Pre-Malignant Lesions
      - Eritroplasia de Queyrat
      - Bowen's Disease (CIS)
      - Leukoplakia
    - Treatment
      - Superficial: 5-FU, Laser, or Circumcision
      - Invasive: Partial or Total Penectomy
      - Linfadenectomy Inguinal
    - Staging
      - Jackson Classification
