## Estrutura do Mapa

**Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs) Part 1**
  - ### General Overview
    - Transmission Routes
      - Sexual (Vaginal, Anal, Oral)
      - Vertical (Mother to Child)
      - Blood (Transfusion, Needles)
      - Direct Contact (Open Lesions)
    - Prevention
      - Condoms
      - Vaccination (HPV, Hep B)
      - Partner Tracking
    - Compulsory Notification
      - Syphilis
      - HIV/AIDS
      - Hepatitis B and C
  - ### Gonorrhea
    - Agent: Neisseria gonorrhoeae
    - Symptoms: Purulent yellow-green discharge
    - Diagnosis: Gram stain (Diplococci) / Thayer-Martin culture
    - Treatment
      - Ceftriaxone 500mg IM
      - Must treat Chlamydia simultaneously
    - Complication: Gonococcal arthritis
  - ### Chlamydia
    - Agent: Chlamydia trachomatis (D-K)
    - Symptoms: Clear/mucoid discharge, often asymptomatic
    - Diagnosis: NAAT (PCR)
    - Treatment
      - Azithromycin 1g VO (Safe for Pregnancy)
      - Doxycycline (Alternative)
    - Complication: Reiter's Syndrome
  - ### Trichomoniasis
    - Agent: Trichomonas vaginalis (Protozoan)
    - Symptoms: Foamy green-yellow discharge, foul odor
    - Clinical Sign: Strawberry cervix
    - Diagnosis: Fresh microscopy (mobile trophozoites), pH > 4.5
    - Treatment: Metronidazole 2g VO
  - ### Syphilis
    - Agent: Treponema pallidum
    - Stages
      - Primary: Painless hard chancre
      - Secondary: Palmar/plantar rash, Condyloma latum
      - Latent: Asymptomatic
      - Tertiary: Gummas, Neurosyphilis, Aortitis
    - Diagnosis
      - VDRL/RPR (Screening and Cure Control)
      - FTA-ABS (Confirmatory)
    - Treatment
      - Penicillin G Benzathine (Standard)
      - Penicillin G Crystalline (Neurosyphilis)
  - ### Classification by Lesion
    - Urethritis: Gonorrhea, Chlamydia
    - Vaginitis: Trichomoniasis
    - Genital Ulcers: Syphilis, Chancroid, Herpes
    - Warts: HPV
