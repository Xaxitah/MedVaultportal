## Estrutura do Mapa

**ITS Part 2: Ulcerative and Systemic Diseases**
  - ### Painful Genital Ulcers
    - Chancroid (Haemophilus ducreyi)
      - Multiple ulcers with dirty/necrotic base
      - Painful bubo (may fistulize)
      - Treatment: Azithromycin 1g or Ceftriaxone
    - Genital Herpes (HSV-2)
      - Multiple vesicles and painful ulcers
      - Latent in sacral ganglion (S2-S4)
      - Treatment: Acyclovir
      - Suppression indicated for >6 episodes/year
  - ### Painless Genital Ulcers
    - Lymphogranuloma Venereum (C. trachomatis L1-3)
      - Transient ulcer (often unnoticed)
      - Sinal do sulco (Groove sign) - Pathognomonic
      - Treatment: Doxycycline 21 days
    - Donovanosis (Klebsiella granulomatis)
      - Vegetative/granulomatous friable ulcer
      - No true lymphadenopathy (pseudobubo)
      - Diagnosis: Donovan Bodies
      - Treatment: Doxycycline 21 days
  - ### HPV (Human Papillomavirus)
    - Low Risk (6, 11)
      - Condyloma acuminatum (cauliflower-like)
    - High Risk (16, 18)
      - Cervical, vulvar, and anal cancer
    - Prevention and Treatment
      - Vaccination (Quadrivalent/Nonavalent)
      - Local destruction (Podophyllin, ATA, Cryotherapy)
  - ### HIV/AIDS
    - Transmission
      - Sexual (Anal receptive highest risk)
      - Blood/Vertical
    - Diagnosis
      - 4th Gen ELISA (p24 + antibodies)
      - Western Blot (confirmatory)
    - Management
      - ART (TDF + 3TC + DTG) for everyone
      - PEP: Start within 72 hours
      - PrEP: Pre-exposure prophylaxis
      - Cotrimoxazole if CD4 < 200
