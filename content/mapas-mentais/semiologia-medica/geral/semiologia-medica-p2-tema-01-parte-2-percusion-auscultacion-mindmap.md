## Estrutura do Mapa

**Respiratory Physical Exam: Percussion and Auscultation**
  - ### Percussion
    - Technique
      - Digito-digital method
      - Pleximeter finger parallel to intercostal spaces
      - Comparative and bilateral
      - Patient seated with scapulas apart
    - Normal Findings
      - Conserved pulmonary sonority
      - Sonorous/Clear lung sound
    - Topographic Variations
      - Hepatic sub-matidez (4th-5th right space)
      - Cardiac sub-matidez (3rd left space)
      - Traube space tympanism (stomach)
    - Clinical Correlates
      - Tympanism: Pneumothorax or Asthma
      - Matidez: Consolidation or Liquid
      - Diaphragmatic excursion (3-6 cm)
  - ### Auscultation
    - General Technique
      - Direct skin contact (no clothing)
      - Comparative planes
      - Listen to inspiration and expiration
      - Quiet environment
    - Normal Breath Sounds
      - Vesicular Murmur
        - Low intensity and grave pitch
        - Heard in peripheral lung fields
      - Glottic Soplo
        - Tubular and high intensity
        - Heard over trachea/large bronchi
      - Bronchovesicular
        - Intermediate sound
        - Interscapular region
    - Voice Auscultation
      - Test with 'Treinta y tres'
      - Normal: Distorted/muffled sound
      - Abnormal: Clear words (Pectoriloquy)
      - Whispered voice (Cuchicheada)
  - ### Normal Registry
    - Conserved pulmonary sonority
    - Vesicular murmur present and audibly conserved
    - No adventitious sounds (estertores)
    - No pectoriloquy
