## Estrutura do Mapa

**Respiratory Physical Exam: Inspection and Palpation**
  - ### General Principles
    - IPPA Sequence
    - Preparation and Privacy
    - Comparative Evaluation
    - Topographic Regions
  - ### Inspection
    - Normal Parameters
      - FR 16-20/min
      - Symmetrical Expansion
      - Regular Rhythm
      - Absence of Effort Signs
    - Signs of Difficulty
      - Aleteo Nasal
      - Cianosis
      - Tiraje
      - Accessory Muscle Use
    - Pathological Patterns
      - Kussmaul (Fast/Deep)
      - Cheyne-Stokes (Apnea Cycles)
  - ### Palpation
    - General Palpation
      - Pain and Masses
      - Subcutaneous Crepitus
    - Thoracic Expansion
      - Vertice-Base Maneuver
      - Thumb Symmetry
    - Vocal Vibrations
      - Treinta y Tres Technique
      - Single Hand Comparison
      - Physiological Variations
  - ### Clinical Diagnosis
    - Respiratory Insufficiency
      - Two or more altered criteria
      - SaO2 < 95%
      - Immediate Oxygen Therapy
