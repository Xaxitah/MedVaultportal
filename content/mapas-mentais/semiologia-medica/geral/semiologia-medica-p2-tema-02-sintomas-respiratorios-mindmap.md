## Estrutura do Mapa

**Respiratory Symptoms (Semiology)**
  - ### Main Motives for Consultation
    - Chest Pain (Dolor Torácico)
      - Pleuritic (Puntada de Costado)
      - Tracheobronchial (Urente)
      - Mediastinal
      - Parietal (Somatic)
    - Dyspnea (Lack of Air)
      - Effort/Exercise
      - Rest/Permanent
      - Paroxysmal
      - Positional (Orthopnea, Platypnea, Trepopnea)
    - Cough (Tos)
      - Dry (Irritative)
      - Humid (Secretion present)
      - Productive (Expectorated)
      - Patterns (Bitonal, Nocturnal, Emetizing)
    - Expectoration (Sputum)
      - Serous (Clear/Pinkish)
      - Mucous (Transparent)
      - Mucopurulent (Yellow/Green)
      - Sanguinous (Blood-streaked)
    - Hemoptysis
      - Lower airway origin
      - Red, frothy blood
      - Differential: Hematemesis, Epistaxis
    - Vomica
      - Abrupt expulsion
      - Large volume (>300mL)
      - Pus or liquid
    - Cyanosis
      - Central (Respiratory)
      - Reduced Hemoglobin increase
      - Bluish skin/mucosa
  - ### Obligatory Anamnesis Questions
    - Smoking status
    - History of Asthma/Lung disease
    - Cooking with wood/coal
    - Chemical exposure
  - ### Syndromic Approach
    - Bronchial Syndromes (Bronchitis, Asthma)
    - Parenchymal Syndromes (Pneumonia, Atelectasis)
    - Pleural Syndromes (Effusion, Pneumothorax)
  - ### Infection Classification
    - Upper Airway (VAS)
      - Above Larynx
      - Self-limiting/Viral
      - Rhinitis, Pharyngitis
    - Lower Airway (VAI)
      - Below Larynx
      - Bronchitis, Pneumonia
