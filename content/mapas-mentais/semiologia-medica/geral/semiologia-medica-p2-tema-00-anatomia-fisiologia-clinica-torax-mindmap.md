## Estrutura do Mapa

**Anatomy and Clinical Physiology of the Thorax**
  - ### Pleural Layers
    - Parietal Pleura
      - Covers chest wall, diaphragm, mediastinum
      - Sensitive to pain
    - Visceral Pleura
      - Covers lungs
      - Minimal pain sensitivity
    - Pleural Cavity
      - Serous fluid space
      - Enables lung sliding
  - ### Pulmonary Anatomy
    - Right Lung
      - Three lobes
      - Short, wide, vertical bronchus
      - High aspiration risk
    - Left Lung
      - Two lobes
    - Functional Unit
      - Respiratory bronchiole
      - Alveolar ducts
      - Alveoli
  - ### Respiratory Mechanics
    - Inspiration
      - Active process
      - Diaphragm and external intercostals
    - Expiration
      - Normal: Passive elastic recoil
      - Forced: Internal intercostals and abdominals
    - Regulation
      - Bulbar respiratory center
      - Surfactant reduces surface tension
      - Hering-Breuer reflex
  - ### Clinical Correlations (IPPA)
    - Chest Wall
      - Inspection and Palpation
    - Parenchyma
      - Percussion and Auscultation
      - Condensation and Atelectasis
    - Bronchi
      - Wheezing and secretion
